# Changelog

## 0.1.0-beta2 (22 November 2026)

- Extended fireworks and the alternating drone display to all 24 hours of New Year’s Day in the visitor’s time zone.
- Added checks for 02:00, noon, the final second of 1 January and the midnight cutoff in Nepal.


## 0.1.0-beta1 (21 November 2026)

- Ported Prodockit Christmas snow, global sleigh route, New Year fireworks and
  drone displays to Joomla's Web Asset Manager and script options.
- Added seasonal settings, saved visitor preferences, reduced-motion handling
  and sound that starts muted.
- Replaced sleigh presents with walking boots tied with red and gold bows.
- Positioned accessible on/off and pause/play icons above Our Next Walks,
  with a fixed corner fallback and optional alternative positions.
- Reduced control glyphs to 18 pixels and made their colours follow the page
  palette, including theme changes while animation is paused.
- Fixed Christmas preview route progression when its diagnostic panel is hidden.
- Added local website mirroring, JavaScript and PHP behaviour checks, and
  documentation of the beta and its remaining Joomla integration checks.

- Documented RA Dark Theme as a recommended prerequisite and added beta download instructions.

## 0.1.0-dev (21 November 2026)

- Initialised the Joomla system plugin, manifest-based packaging and CI.
- Added Zensical documentation with Prodockit formatting and managed assets.
